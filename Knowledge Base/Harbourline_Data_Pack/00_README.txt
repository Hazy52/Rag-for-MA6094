HOW TO USE IT
  - Together with the project brief and posted sponsor answers, this pack is the
    ONLY source your memory, risk and reporting modules may draw on.
  - The files were written by different people at different times. They do not
    always agree with each other or with the brief. When they conflict, your system
    should say so and cite both sources, not silently pick one.
  - Cite file name and meeting, page, row ID or section when your system uses a fact.
  - CSV files are UTF-8. Some consultation comments are in Chinese, Malay or Tamil.
