LESSONS LEARNED — SEABREEZE COMMUNITY HUB REFURBISHMENT (2019–2021)
Prepared by the Seabreeze Town Council project team, close-out report, June 2021.
Shared with Harbourline Town Council for reference.

Project summary: 7,200 m2 hub, 1991 building. Budget S$12.8M, final cost S$14.1M.
Planned 18 months, actual 23 months. Hub kept open throughout.

L01 Keeping the hub open
    Keeping all floors open added about 3 months and S$0.6M (temporary works,
    night shifts, resequencing). Residents later said a short, well-announced
    closure would have been preferable to 18 months of partial disruption.
    Recommendation: agree closures with the sponsor before contract award.

L02 Noise
    31 formal noise complaints, 22 of them in months 4–7 during hacking works.
    Complaints fell once a weekly works notice was posted at lift lobbies.

L03 Hazardous materials
    Asbestos was found in ceiling boards in month 3 despite a desk study saying none
    was expected. Removal added 5 weeks. Recommendation: commission an intrusive
    hazardous-materials survey for any pre-2000 building before tender.

L04 Childcare licensing
    The childcare operator needed about 7 months between floor handover and first
    intake (fit-out, inspections, licence). The project had allowed 3.

L05 Late operator appointment
    The senior activity centre operator was appointed in month 14, after the layout
    was fixed. Two rooms had to be reconfigured at a cost of S$85,000.

L06 Consultation
    The online survey drew 80% of responses, mostly from residents under 45.
    Seniors, the main users of two of the new facilities, were under-represented.
    A second round with door-to-door and multilingual paper forms changed the
    priority order of three features.

L07 Reporting
    Weekly written status reports to the committee were rarely read in full.
    From month 9 the team switched to a one-page exception report: what changed,
    what is late, what needs a decision. Committee time on decisions doubled.

L08 Grant claims
    One grant tranche was delayed 4 months because a condition (signed operator
    agreements) had been missed in the project schedule. Recommendation: put every
    grant condition into the schedule as a milestone with an owner.
