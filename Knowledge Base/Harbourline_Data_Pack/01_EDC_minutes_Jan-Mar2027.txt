HARBOURLINE TOWN COUNCIL
ESTATE DEVELOPMENT COMMITTEE (EDC) — MINUTES, HARBOURLINE COMMUNITY HUB REFURBISHMENT
Extract: six meetings, January to March 2027

Standing attendees: Daniel Koh (Director, Estate Development, Chair), Mei Ling Ong
(Finance Manager), Ravi Shankar (Project Manager, Council), Farah Aziz (Community
Engagement Lead), Kestrel Harbour Builders (KHB) represented by Jonathan Lim (Project
Director). Others as listed.

======================================================================
MEETING 1 — Thursday 7 January 2027
======================================================================
1.1 Contract award. The design-and-build contract was awarded to Kestrel Harbour
    Builders Pte Ltd on 18 December 2026 at a contract sum of S$16.42 million.
    Site possession given Monday 4 January 2027. Consultancy and authority fees are
    estimated at S$0.90 million, held outside the contract.
1.2 Ms Ong noted the approved budget of S$18.5 million leaves about S$1.18 million
    once fees are deducted, and asked whether this is to be treated as contingency.
    No decision recorded.
1.3 The Chair reaffirmed the Council's commitment that the Hub REMAINS OPEN to
    residents throughout the works. KHB to submit a phasing plan by 1 February.
1.4 Grant conditions from the Neighbourhood Renewal Fund (NRF) not yet received.
    ACTION: D. Koh to obtain by 15 January.
1.5 Active ageing centre operator: expression of interest (EOI) to be issued in
    February. ACTION: R. Shankar.
1.6 The EDC will meet weekly on Thursdays for the duration of the works.

======================================================================
MEETING 2 — Thursday 21 January 2027
======================================================================
Additional attendee: Grace Teo (Little Harbour Learning, Centre Development Manager)
2.1 NRF grant conditions received 19 January (see file 07). Ms Ong reported that the
    Fund caps ELIGIBLE cost at S$17.0 million and the 60% grant applies to eligible
    cost only. The food court refresh and rooftop community garden are listed as
    ineligible. Ms Ong to report the funding effect at the next meeting.
2.2 Ms Teo confirmed Little Harbour Learning intends to open enrolment in July 2028
    and admit the first children in January 2029. She asked for handover of the
    childcare floor "by month 18" to complete fit-out and licensing.
2.3 To reduce load on members, the EDC will meet FORTNIGHTLY from February. Written
    updates may be circulated in alternate weeks.
2.4 KHB reported mobilisation complete; site office established in the car park.
2.5 Meeting 1 action 1.4 closed.

======================================================================
MEETING 3 — Thursday 4 February 2027
======================================================================
3.1 KHB phasing plan received. KHB advised that:
    (a) replacing the two lifts in the east core requires closing the east core for
        about 14 weeks, during which levels 3 and 4 have no barrier-free access;
    (b) the sports hall roof and M&E replacement needs the hall closed for about
        10 weeks;
    (c) the food court must close for 6 weeks for kitchen exhaust and grease-trap works.
3.2 The Chair stated the Hub must remain open and asked KHB to "resequence where
    possible". Mr Lim said a temporary lift is possible but not priced. No decision.
3.3 Resident consultation launched online on 1 February, closing 28 February. Paper
    forms available at the Hub counter. One town hall to be held on 13 February.
3.4 Active ageing operator EOI issued 2 February, closing 26 February.
3.5 Ms Ong reported the funding effect of item 2.1 (figures in her paper, not minuted).

======================================================================
MEETING 4 — Thursday 25 February 2027
======================================================================
4.1 KHB baseline programme submitted. Key dates: practical completion
    31 December 2028; childcare floor handover to operator for fit-out
    month 20 (August 2028); phased reopening of sports hall from month 14.
4.2 The Chair queried how a 31 December 2028 completion fits the "fully open by
    Q4 2028" commitment. Mr Shankar to reconcile and report.
4.3 Ms Teo (by email, read out) noted an August 2028 handover does not allow the
    licensing period her organisation needs before a January 2029 intake.
4.4 Active ageing operator EOI: one response received. Council procurement policy
    requires at least three. EOI extended to 30 April 2027.
4.5 Town hall on 13 February attended by about 40 residents.

======================================================================
MEETING 5 — Thursday 11 March 2027
======================================================================
5.1 KHB reported suspected asbestos-containing ceiling boards on level 2 (west wing)
    during soft-strip. Works in that zone stopped on 3 March. Specialist survey
    commissioned; KHB estimates removal at S$0.35–0.60 million and 4–6 weeks.
    The 2025 feasibility study had stated no hazardous materials were expected.
5.2 Structural engineer's note: the roof slab needs strengthening before it can carry
    the rooftop garden soil load. Cost not yet estimated.
5.3 Resident consultation closed 28 February with 214 responses recorded.
    Ms Aziz to present a summary at Meeting 6.
5.4 Mr Shankar has not yet reconciled the programme (action 4.2). Carried forward.

======================================================================
MEETING 6 — Thursday 25 March 2027
======================================================================
6.1 NRF Tranche 1 claim submitted 19 March.
6.2 Ms Ong drew attention to the Tranche 2 condition requiring signed operator
    agreements for ALL social service spaces. The active ageing operator is not yet
    appointed (see 4.4).
6.3 A proposal to defer the rooftop garden to a later phase was discussed, given
    items 2.1 and 5.2. Members were divided. The Chair will "take a view with the
    sponsor" — no decision recorded.
6.4 Consultation summary postponed to Meeting 7; Ms Aziz still cleaning the data.
6.5 Asbestos survey confirmed chrysotile in level 2 ceiling boards. Variation order
    to be raised by KHB. Removal sequence not yet agreed.
6.6 Next meeting: Thursday 8 April 2027.
