NEIGHBOURHOOD RENEWAL FUND (NRF) — LETTER OF GRANT CONDITIONS (EXTRACT)
Recipient: Harbourline Town Council
Project: Harbourline Community Hub Refurbishment
Reference: NRF/2026/HCH-017 · Issued 19 January 2027

1. GRANT AMOUNT
   1.1 The grant is 60% of eligible project cost.
   1.2 Eligible project cost is capped at S$17.0 million. Maximum grant S$10.2 million.
   1.3 Ineligible items include: refurbishment of commercial premises (including food
       courts and leased retail), and landscaping or rooftop amenity not forming part
       of a social service facility.
   1.4 Cost increases above the cap are borne by the recipient.

2. TRANCHES
   | Tranche | Share of grant | Release condition                                                    |
   |---------|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
   | T1      | 20%            | Contract award and submission of baseline programme                  |
   | T2      | 30%            | Completion of structural and M&E rough-in, AND signed operator        |
   |         |                | agreements for ALL social service spaces in the facility             |
   | T3      | 30%            | Temporary Occupation Permit (TOP) for the whole facility              |
   | T4      | 20%            | Six months of operation and submission of a utilisation report        |

3. REPORTING
   3.1 A monthly progress report is due by the 10th working day of each month.
   3.2 It must state: progress against the baseline programme, expenditure to date
       against eligible cost, status of each tranche condition, and top five risks.
   3.3 Two consecutive late or incomplete reports may suspend the next tranche.

4. CHANGES
   4.1 Any change to the scope of social service facilities (childcare, active ageing,
       accessibility works) requires the Fund's prior written approval.
   4.2 Completion later than 31 December 2028 requires the Fund's prior approval.

5. ACCESSIBILITY
   5.1 Barrier-free access must be maintained to all floors in public use during
       the works, unless the Fund agrees otherwise in writing.
